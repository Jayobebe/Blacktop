# Card Wars rules

- Keep Card Wars round events in step between `lib/events.ts` (order, odds, numbers) and `cw_action` (event index + 1 in the log); a rapture restores the card server-side (player battles always, computer battles at most once a day), so a forged report gains little.
- Card sales go only through the `cw_trade_*` server functions, with offered RPM held in escrow and refunded on decline, cancel, expiry or delete, so cards and RPM always move together.
- Card-for-card swaps settle only in `cw_swap_ready` once both sides are ready, re-checking ownership and balances under lock; any slot change resets both, so nobody can switch what's offered.
- Own and scanned cards battle with `lib/ownRatings.ts` on the nearest Road card of their kind (`lib/ownMatch.ts`, never a hash of the id), plus the riding gap scaled by tier, at most 12 a rating and capped at the catalogue's best per category and overall; player battles send only the finished ratings, which the server bounds and locks for 7 days in `cw_own_cards`.
- Daily contracts and maker sets are server-held (`cw_contracts`, `cw_contract_report`, `cw_sets`, `cw_set_claim`); battles report facts once per battle id, capped per day, so a forged report can earn at most a day's contracts.
- A deck carries three dog tags of the four powers (`TAG_SLOTS`, `POWERS` order is the server's `_tag` index): the app sends `-` for the power left at home and `cw_action` refuses a fourth tag or a round played with the missing one; keep Coin flip's sums in step between `lib/tagRules.ts` (`flipCategory`, heads chance) and `cw_tag_value(3, …)` / the flip block in `cw_action`.
- Card wear is counted per round fought, never per battle (`wearLoss` in `lib/rules.ts` = `cw_wear_loss`): the server counts player battles from its own log and takes computer battles' rounds through `cw_report_wear`; change the numbers on both sides together, and keep `cw_save_wear` (flat) only for apps that haven't updated.
- Dog tag builds: any three tags to a deck, sent to player battles as three `power:ref` slots (`slotRef`), with `cw_check_slots` refusing an unowned tag or the same tag twice; keep the older one-entry-per-power form working until every app has updated. A Spectre's tag (`~all`) takes its power from the vault's wheel, once, and its bonus with any vehicle. RPM from the other arcade games goes only through `cw_arcade_reward` (daily cap server-side).
